## The one check that could change the paper

Reference 7 (Vaishnav et al.) uses the same "information per unit energy" criterion as the paper's
Eq. (8). The paper's novelty claim rests on its own model having a fixed per-upload radio cost (the
tail). If Vaishnav et al.'s energy model also has a fixed per-upload cost, the Gap paragraph in
Section II and the Introduction must be narrowed further. Check this first.
